How to use this in practice

Start with the quick pick. For zero-cost experiments, use Gemini 4 Hydrogen's free tier, the free Gemma 4 31B endpoints, or a self-hosted Qwen3.8-27B before paying for frontier models, and use the router for compound workflows. Compare cost per finished task, not per-token price: on the same Artificial Analysis run GPT-6.2 Sol cost $1.01 per task and Claude Sonnet 5.5 at max effort $10.02 — at an identical $2/$10 sticker. Watch three dated prices: Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon double to $4/$20 on Dec 1, GPT-5.6 Sol's promotional price ends Nov 30, and Gemini 3.8 Flash doubles on Jan 1, 2027. Every score here uses the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1. The index was rebuilt on Oct 27 around mostly private test sets and the same models moved by anything from +4 to −16 points, so do not compare these numbers with the v4.3 scores this page showed before.
