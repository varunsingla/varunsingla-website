How to use this in practice

Start with the quick pick. For zero-cost experiments, try Qwen3.8-27B (free OpenRouter tier) or self-host Gemma 4 / GLM-5.3-Flash before paying for frontier models, and use the router for compound workflows. Compare cost per finished task, not per-token price — Grok 4.7 and Sonnet 5.5 at max effort show how the same sticker price can double the bill. Watch: Gemini 4 Argon and Claude Haiku 5.5 are the next likely shake-ups; near-frontier pricing has converged at ~$2/$10 (GPT-6.1 Sol, Sonnet 5.5), and the open-weight lead now sits with MiMo-V2.6-Pro, GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.8.
