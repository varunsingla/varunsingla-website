Takeaways of the week

  • Track where your content and data come from. Know the license of anything an agent retrieves, since per-use payment standards are emerging. Day 207
  • Use suppliers as leading indicators. ASML orders and guidance hint at chip capacity years ahead of product launches. Day 207
  • Limit what agents can reach. Restrict network egress and credentials so a misbehaving agent cannot copy files or itself out. Day 206
  • Read headlines for the mechanism. Ask what the agent actually did, in what environment, and who ran the test. Day 206
  • Read active, not total, parameters. For MoE models, active parameters drive latency and cost; total parameters drive memory. Day 205
  • Wait for the license. Open weights are only useful if the license allows your use; check it before planning a migration. Day 205
  • Measure cost per task, not per token. Count tokens used, retries and the effort setting; a cheaper model is not always a cheaper job. Day 204
  • Check pricing tiers. Look for context-length thresholds (like 100K) and tokenizer changes before trusting a '75% cheaper' claim. Day 204
